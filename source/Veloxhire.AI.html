How it works

From job post to shortlist in days, not weeks.

1
Create your assessment
Select the skills, experience, and traits you need. Veloxhire auto-generates role-specific interview questions — or bring your own.
2
AI conducts the interview
Invite candidates with a single click. The AI holds a full video interview — probing, following up, and assessing in real time, any hour of the day.
3
Review your top 10%
Get a ranked shortlist with detailed reports per candidate — scores, strengths, red flags, and a hiring recommendation. No screening calls required.
4
Make the offer & hire
You make the final call. Veloxhire handled everything else — scheduling, no-shows, and shortlisting. Close the position in days.
