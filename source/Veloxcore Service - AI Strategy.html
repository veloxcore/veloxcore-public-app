Our approach

Two weeks. Three phases. One deliverable.

01
Intake & discovery
Structured interviews with your team — engineering, product, data, and business stakeholders. We map your data landscape, existing systems, and current AI experiments. We collect the raw use case list.
Days 1–3
02
Scoring & prototyping
We score each use case on four axes. For the top 2–3, we build working prototypes with real data — not slide mockups. This is where we find out what's actually feasible vs. what sounds good in a meeting.
Days 4–8
03
Architecture & planning
For each recommended build, we write an architecture decision record: model selection, infrastructure, data requirements, integration plan, and estimated effort. No ambiguity about what "build this" actually means.
Days 9–11
04
Readout & handover
A 3-hour readout with your leadership team — prioritised use case backlog, prototype demos, architecture decisions, 90-day sprint plan, and data/infrastructure readiness report. Everything in writing before we leave.
Day 14

FAQ

Common questions.

Questions we get before every discovery sprint.

What's the difference between this and a regular consulting engagement?
Consultants produce recommendations. We produce working prototypes and architecture decision records that your engineering team can act on immediately. The sprint is run by the same engineers who will build the production system — not a strategy team that hands off to a delivery team. You leave with code, not slides.
Do we need to have our data ready before the sprint?
No — assessing your data readiness is part of the sprint. Many teams start the sprint thinking their data is ready and discover gaps. That's a feature, not a bug: it's better to discover data problems in a 2-week sprint than 6 months into a build. We need read access to a representative sample of your data during the sprint, not a fully prepared dataset.
What happens after the sprint?
You own the output — prototypes, architecture documents, and the sprint plan. You can build with your own team, hand off to another vendor, or engage Veloxcore to build. Most clients continue with us because the sprint creates a shared understanding that makes the subsequent build much faster. The Strategy Sprint includes 30 days of post-sprint engineering support.
How do you handle our data security during the sprint?
We work within your Azure tenant — we don't move your data to our infrastructure. For the sprint, we need temporary read access to data sources (via managed identity, not credentials). All prototypes are built and run inside your subscription. We sign an NDA and a data processing agreement before any access is granted.
