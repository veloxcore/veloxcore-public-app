Context

Agents handling 40% of support tickets — without humans

Our client — a leading SaaS ticketing platform serving 2,000+ enterprises — was drowning in support volume. The support team was processing 15,000+ tickets a month manually. Response time averaged 18 hours for non-critical issues. The CEO mandated a 50% reduction in MTTR without hiring.

We built an AI agent that reads incoming tickets, queries the knowledge base, drafts responses with citations, and routes complex cases to humans with full context. No hallucinations, no made-up answers — every response is grounded in documented knowledge and evaluated before sending.

The challenge

Three hard constraints in support automation

  • Zero hallucinations: A made-up technical answer in a support ticket destroys trust with enterprise customers. The system had to cite sources for every statement.
  • Tone consistency: The platform has its own support voice — helpful, direct, not marketing-speak. The agent had to match it exactly.
  • Escalation routing: Complex issues need to go to senior engineers, not just any human. The system had to classify intent, urgency, and required expertise.
Architecture

RAG + LLM + human-in-the-loop evaluation

The pipeline: incoming ticket → intent classification → knowledge base retrieval (hybrid BM25 + semantic) → LLM draft response → citation verification → tone check → auto-send or escalate to human.

Every response is evaluated against four gates: (1) Citation compliance — does every claim cite a document? (2) Tone match — does it match the platform's support voice? (3) Confidence — is the LLM confident in the answer? (4) Escalation criteria — is this something humans should see? Only responses passing all gates are auto-sent; others are drafted for human review.
