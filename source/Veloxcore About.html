Our story

Built for depth, not breadth.

Veloxcore was founded in 2013 by a team of engineers who had spent years inside enterprise data teams, watching the same problems repeat: pipelines that broke silently, dashboards that were three days stale, ML models that never made it to production. We started the company to fix that — not with a new tool, but with better engineering practice.

For the first seven years, we focused entirely on the Microsoft Azure data platform — Event Hubs, HDInsight, Data Factory, Stream Analytics, Power BI. We went deep. Gold Partner status. Certified engineers. Dozens of enterprise migrations. The kind of depth that comes from doing the same work, again and again, for demanding clients with real SLAs.

In 2023, the world changed. LLMs became production-viable, and every enterprise client we served started asking the same question: how do we actually ship AI? We had a clear answer — because we had the data infrastructure, the Azure depth, and the engineering discipline to build AI systems that work in production, not just in demos. We also decided to prove it by building our own: Veloxhire.AI, launched in 2024, now used by over 100 companies to run AI video interviews at scale.

That's who we are. An engineering studio that ships AI for serious companies — and eats its own cooking.

How we think.

Six principles that govern every engagement, every line of code, every conversation with a client.

01
Production over demos
An AI model that works in a Colab notebook is not an AI product. We measure ourselves by what's running in production at scale — not by what looked good in the presentation.
02
Honest engineering
We tell clients when a problem doesn't need LLMs. We tell clients when their data isn't ready. We'd rather lose a deal than set up an engagement we can't deliver.
03
Depth, not breadth
We don't work in every cloud. We don't support every ML framework. We go very deep on Azure and the Microsoft AI ecosystem because depth compounds in ways that breadth doesn't.
04
We eat our own cooking
Veloxhire.AI is built on the same Azure infrastructure we build for clients. If we recommend a pattern, we've run it in production ourselves. That's not marketing — it's accountability.
05
Your team, not our dependency
Every engagement ends with the client's team owning what we built. Runbooks, documentation, training — the goal is always a handover that doesn't require us to exist.
06
Cost is a feature
Cloud cost is part of every architecture decision, not an afterthought. We've reduced client bills by 30–40% post-engagement — because we build systems that are efficient, not just functional.
