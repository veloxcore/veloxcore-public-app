## The problem

Before PIA, getting a straight answer about last night's GOP meant opening four different modules inside the PMI platform, applying the right filter combination, and waiting for exports to load. General Managers at properties across the group were spending three to four hours per week just navigating reports — time taken away from running the property.

The friction had four forms. **Manual navigation overload**: P&L, forecasts, GoGreen, and KPI data lived in separate screens with no unified view. **Support ticket drain**: a large share of incoming tickets were "how do I find X" questions — process queries, not real problems. **Silent data errors**: unmapped accounts and TKS departments skewed reports without any visible warning. And **context-switching cost**: by the time a GM had pulled the right numbers, the morning briefing had already started.

> "By the time I'd pulled the right filter combination for our weekend F&B numbers, the morning briefing was already happening. I needed answers, not exports."
>
> — General Manager, Flagship Property · International Hotel Group · 250+ keys

## The solution

PIA (PMI Intelligent Assistant) is a conversational AI layer embedded directly in the PMI platform. GMs type questions in plain English — "What's my GOP vs. forecast this month?" — and receive streamed, cited answers in seconds, without leaving the platform.

Six capabilities make up the system. **Instant Q&A** draws on live data and platform context to answer management questions conversationally. **Semantic knowledge search** indexes over 200 PMI knowledge articles with vector embeddings, surfacing citation links alongside every answer. **Module-scoped query templates** for Budget and Forecast, GoGreen, Live Forecast, and KPI Targets reduce the question to a single tap. **Screenshot and file analysis** lets GMs upload a P&L PDF, an Excel export, or a chart screenshot — GPT-4.1 Vision interprets and explains it. **Proactive data-quality alerts** surface unmapped accounts, pending TKS mappings, and stale import status in-chat before a GM acts on incomplete data. And **smart support escalation** raises a ticket with the full conversation attached when PIA can't resolve an issue — one click, no context lost.

## How it works

When a GM submits a question, the current PMI module context (Budget, GoGreen, Forecast) is automatically passed with the query. GPT-4.1 classifies the intent in milliseconds — general Q&A, import status check, mapping check, or support request — and routes to the right handler. Azure AI Search performs semantic and vector search across the knowledge base, filtered to the GM's active modules and property. GPT-4.1 then streams the answer character by character via Server-Sent Events, with citations linking back to source articles. Chat history is preserved in Cosmos DB per user. If the response doesn't resolve the issue, one click raises a support ticket with the full conversation pre-attached.

## Results

Outcomes measured over the first 90 days across participating properties: GMs reclaimed **3 hours per week** previously spent navigating modules and waiting for exports. **87% of support queries** were self-served by PIA's knowledge base without reaching the support team. Time to first answer dropped from 20+ minutes to **under 5 seconds**. GMs reported arriving at morning briefings with GOP, occupancy, and F&B numbers already verified — pulled conversationally.

## Admin controls

Chain-level admins configure PIA without developer involvement. GPT temperature, response length, nucleus sampling, and context window size (1–10 past messages) are all adjustable in the admin panel. System prompts, query library templates, welcome messages, and image analysis prompts are configurable per module and per property. Usage reporting exports to Excel by property, user, and month — including token counts and query volumes — for any date range.
