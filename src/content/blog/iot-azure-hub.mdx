## Devices are not the hard part

The hard part of IoT was never the devices. The hard part is the boundary — the contract between the device fleet and the cloud, defined precisely enough that both halves can evolve independently.

## What IoT Hub actually gives you

Bidirectional messaging with at-least-once guarantees. Device twins. Module twins for edge workloads. Identity per device. A built-in routing system that can split telemetry across multiple downstream consumers.

> Most IoT projects fail at the protocol layer or the identity layer. IoT Hub gets both right, which is why even teams who don't love Azure choose it for the boundary.

## Where the value compounds

Once IoT Hub is in place, IoT Edge becomes trivial — same identity, same twin model, same routing — but now your inference runs on the device.
