<p id="intro">For most enterprises, "we run Hadoop" used to mean a dedicated platform team, a permanent cluster, and a queue of jobs nobody wanted to touch. HDInsight removes the cluster as a permanent artefact — and that single change cascades through every operational decision downstream.</p>

## Why managed Hadoop matters

The promise of HDInsight is straightforward: a fully managed Hadoop, Spark, HBase, or Kafka cluster, provisioned in minutes, scaled on demand, torn down when the workload finishes.

<h2 id="ops">Operational tradeoffs</h2>

You lose some control. You can't ship a custom kernel. You can't pin to a specific Spark patch level. But for 95% of teams, those constraints are features, not bugs.

> The best ops decision a data team can make is to give up the parts of ops that don't differentiate them.

<h2 id="cost">Cost model</h2>

Pay for what runs. A 200-node cluster that exists for 90 minutes is dramatically cheaper than a 50-node cluster that exists 24/7.
